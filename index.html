D3 Civil Engineering graduate · Politeknik Raflesia

I am a D3 Civil Engineering graduate of Politeknik Raflesia with a strong interest in technical drawing and BIM modeling. I developed this interest through an internship as an AutoCAD Drafter at PT Citra Utama Conindo, where I created and revised working drawings for a residential house and an official residence under an engineer's direction. During my field work practice at PT Angkasa Pura Indonesia, I was also involved in airport facility inspections and technical drawing. For my final project, I used Autodesk Revit to model a five-story office building and integrated the model with structural analysis in Robot Structural Analysis. These experiences drew me to design coordination, working drawing development, and the use of BIM in construction projects. I want to keep deepening these skills through work involving shop drawings, drawing documentation, and technical coordination with engineering teams.
